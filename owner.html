1. Order Management

View incoming tickets in real-time. Use the tabs to filter by Active, Fulfilled, or Canceled orders.

  • Mark Ready: Triggers an alert (and SMS if enabled) letting customers know their food is hot.
  • Fulfill: Clears the order from the active queue after pickup and adds the revenue to your analytics.
  • Cancel: Voids the order and moves it to the canceled tab.

2. Live Inventory Control

Changes made here sync instantly via SSE to the live customer ordering page.

  • Edit: Instantly change names, prices, sale prices, and prep times.
  • Instant Removal: Use the toggle switch to mark an item 'Sold Out'. It will gray out for customers instantly.

3. Settings & Operations

  • Operational Status: Instantly close the store to disable checkout on the customer portal.
  • Rush Mode: Append 15 or 30 minutes to all calculated wait times during a busy rush. Shows a warning banner to users.
  • Location Manager: Set your active parking spot so the storefront maps customers accurately.

4. Analytics & Time Clock

  • Dashboard: Track real-time financial data, gross revenue, and your top-selling items based on fulfilled orders.
  • Time Clock: Access employee hours via the pill icon in the top right header.
